# Product

## Register

product

## Users

SakhiCircle serves Indian women aged 45 and above, including homemakers, working women, empty nesters, and retirees. They may be returning to a postponed hobby, learning a new skill, seeking peers at a similar life stage, or sharing lifelong knowledge as a mentor. SakhiCircle is a mobile-first progressive web application that works in a phone or desktop browser. Users may prefer Hindi to English and may be less comfortable with typing or dense digital interfaces.

Their primary job is to move from an intention—“I want to begin again”—to a realistic learning routine, a trusted peer group, and, when relevant, a credible way to teach and earn.

## Product Purpose

SakhiCircle is a voice-first web learning and mentorship platform where women can learn, teach, earn, and belong. Its judged MVP turns a spoken or typed aspiration into an editable four-week learning journey, makes one explainable peer or mentor recommendation, and records pseudonymous engagement outcomes. Circles, production payments, and native distribution remain later phases.

Success means a learner can complete the full journey from onboarding to plan, match, circle, mentor booking, and progress update without needing technical assistance. The product must also make skills transferred, learning engagement, and mentor income measurable without turning private user activity into surveillance.

For every confirmed learning topic that is safe for external discovery, a reviewed journey attempts to find a suitable sequential YouTube playlist, divide one or two verified videos across each chosen week, and explain what to prepare, notice, practise, and expect. The preferred plan language is tried first and any fallback is labelled. Video guidance enriches rather than replaces the complete written plan; an unavailable or unsuitable playlist never blocks it.

## Brand Personality

Dignified, encouraging, rooted.

The product should feel like a capable companion that respects the user’s experience. It is warm without becoming sentimental, culturally aware without becoming ornamental, and technologically confident without foregrounding the AI.

## Anti-references

- A generic social-media feed or hobby marketplace where attention and popularity replace purposeful learning.
- Infantilizing “senior wellness” products that use patronizing copy, oversized decoration, or assumptions about frailty.
- Clinical mental-health dashboards or medicalized claims about loneliness and well-being.
- Voice-only experiences that hide transcripts, remove user control, or fail when speech input is unavailable.
- Dense enterprise dashboards with tiny text, weak contrast, unexplained scores, or complex navigation.

## Design Principles

- **Respect before delight.** Treat users as experienced adults; never simplify by removing agency or context.
- **One meaningful next step.** Every screen should make the next action obvious without hiding secondary information.
- **Voice with visible control.** Voice accelerates input, while transcripts, confirmation, editing, and text alternatives preserve trust.
- **Explain the intelligence.** Plans, matches, adaptations, and engagement signals show their factors and require confirmation before consequential changes.
- **Celebrate progress, not performance.** Reinforce consistency, skill transfer, and mutual support without public popularity mechanics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The product must meet WCAG 2.2 AA with high legibility beyond the minimum: 18px-equivalent default body text, 48×48 CSS-pixel targets, visible keyboard focus, screen-reader names, zoom and text scaling to 200%, reduced-motion behavior, and contrast-safe semantic states. English and Hindi are first-class interface languages, not machine-translated afterthoughts. Every voice interaction has a complete text alternative, and no raw voice recording is retained.
